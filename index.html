About · How these connect

I observe people. I understand the context. I turn an idea into something you can see. Then I make it real — on a screen, on paper, or in a room.

That is the whole method. Only the rooms change: a dental lab in New York, a bike shop in Vancouver, an art academy in Korea, a nail bar downtown. Each one taught me a different way to see the person on the other side.
